# PLAN — H1+ toward the 1800 target (written 2026-09-18 ~20:30 CEST)

Context: deployed controller H1 measures ~7,500 ticks locally (official validation 768.68,
i.e. score == simulated time in seconds). Pooled over 60 paired held-out seeds H1 beats the
old controller by +380 ticks (+5.4%), but block-by-block it ranges -0.2% to +12.1%: it is a
real but MODEST gain. Target is ~18,000 ticks (score 1800), i.e. 2.4x what we have.

## What the source code says (measured today, not assumed)

- `score = self.time` — the sim adds `dt` (0.1) per tick, so score == ticks/10. Fruit adds
  only `energy/1000` (0.06 for a full fruit) and being eaten costs `energy/100`, so survival time
  DOMINATES the score, with small energy/fruit terms on top (a full fruit is +0.06 against
  0.1/tick; being eaten costs up to -5). Nothing rewards fleet size directly.
- There is NO episode cap in the environment: a run ends when the last agent dies.
- The world DECAYS: `tree_spawn_chance *= 0.5 ** (time/300)` — production halves every 300
  simulated seconds (3,000 ticks). Fruit itself rots at age 100 (1,000 ticks). So late game increasingly rewards
  conserved reserves while food availability AND access become more constrained -- earlier
  measurements found standing fruit remaining in the world while agents struggled to reach it,
  so the access side matters as much as the supply side.
- Agents age out: `max_age = 60 + U(0,60)` seconds = 600-1,200 ticks, then `energy -= 0.01*age`
  per tick. Survival beyond one lifetime is a RELAY: each generation must hand off to an heir
  (spawn costs 100 energy, child starts with 75).
- Movement: walk 10 units at 0.05/unit = 0.5/tick. SPRINT 20 units costs
  `10*0.05 + 10*0.5` = 5.5/tick (11x metabolism). Turning costs `|turn|/(2pi)`.
- **THE LOCKOUT (the key discovery):** `update_entity_position` clamps distance to the WALK
  speed whenever `energy < max_energy/5` (i.e. < 100 energy). An agent below 100 energy
  physically CANNOT sprint.
- Predators: speed 11, sprint 15, energy 0-200, start asleep, wake at >100, `energy += 3/tick`
  while resting and CANNOT kill while resting (the `continue` skips the interaction block).
  They drain to 0 and fall asleep again. They only CHARGE when `|agent_looking_dir| > pi/2`
  (the prey is not looking at them) OR distance < 90; otherwise they PIVOT 45 degrees off, which
  closes radially at 15*cos(45) = 10.6/tick.
- **Therefore:** agent sprint 20 > predator sprint 15, so an agent CAN be run away from — but
  only while it holds 100+ energy. A low-energy agent walks at 10 < 15 and is run down. That is a
  CANDIDATE mechanical reason 35-43% of deaths are predation: births cost 100, so the fleet may
  live near the lockout threshold. Whether it DOMINATES the other explanations (predator
  avoidance, food accessibility, movement expenditure, population structure, reproduction
  timing, and their interactions) is exactly what the lockout experiment and the diagnostic
  decide. Do NOT conclude that it explains the ~7k ceiling until they do.
- Biomes are OBSERVABLE (`biome` is a type string in the agent state) and differ: grass/forest
  fruit_spawn_rate 0.1, swamp 0.08 with move_penalty 0.5, desert 0.05 with 0.8, river 0.0 with
  0.3. energy_drain_rate is 1.0 everywhere, so there is no low-drain refuge — only good and bad
  foraging ground.

## Research: how others solve this class of problem

1. **SELFish (Hahn et al. 2019, ALIFE)** — prey learned with a reward of "+1 per step alive,
   -1000 when caught", i.e. exactly our score. Emergent result: flocking. But the paper's own
   control measurement: **for an individual's survival the best policy was simply TurnAway —
   turn from the predator and run** — and the learned swarm did NOT find it, because staying in
   the group was a self-interested Nash equilibrium. Lesson: a rule-based escape beats hoping
   the learner discovers it, and our reward is already the survival reward they used.
2. **Predator-prey survival pressure is sufficient to evolve swarming (NJP 2023)** — reward of
   -1 for being caught, nothing else, produced flocking, dispersion, confusion and marginal
   predation. Lesson: no hand-crafted anti-predator term is needed; survival pressure alone
   expresses the behaviour — so the objective does not need reshaping.
3. **Dilution of risk / selfish herd (arXiv 2209.06338; PMC2169279)** — "dilution of risk is
   sufficient to evolve group formation"; most hunted members "are frequently those who break
   away". Selfish-herd theory: risk is highest on the PERIPHERY, so an individual's best move
   is to put a conspecific between itself and the predator. IMPORTANT: this is a hypothesis to
   TEST here, not an established result for this simulator — the literature does not show that
   any specific formation is optimal in our world. What the literature does justify is that our
   controller lacks any positioning term relative to the predator, which is what makes it worth
   an arm.
- **MAP-Elites with adaptive sampling (GECCO 2019)** — QD maintains a repertoire of diverse
   high performers and re-samples elites. Honest justification: the evaluation is DETERMINISTIC
   inside a fixed harness (the object-ordering fix reproduces 462.3278 exactly), so the dominant
   variance is SEED-TO-SEED outcome variance, not stochastic evaluation noise. QD is adopted for
   DIVERSITY and ROBUSTNESS ACROSS SEEDS (and because it keeps creative alternatives alive, a
   user requirement) — not because the simulator is noisy.

## Predicted effect sizes (vs a measured +-1,000-tick noise floor)

- A. Evasion geometry (retreat-while-facing at sprint, keep 100+ reserve) — the lockout means
  prey die because they cannot sprint. Predicted +2,000-6,000 ticks. HIGHEST, resolvable.
- B. Endgame thin relay (spawn only a well-fed heir, keep <=3 agents, from a reachable trigger)
  — the world decays so the endgame is a bank; earlier refutation used an unreachable trigger
  and a gate that blocked births. Predicted +1,000-3,000.
- C. Biome-aware foraging (prefer forest/grassland, avoid swamp/river when blind) — cheap,
  predicted +300-800 (may be absorbed by existing behaviour).
- D. Selfish-herd positioning / dilution — literature-backed, predicted +500-1,500.
- E. QD search — not an effect, but the only way to tune A-D jointly under this noise.
- F. RL evasion sub-policy (hybrid spawn) — user asks for ML; time-boxed, kill-criterion stated.

   >500 ticks on >=20 PAIRED held-out seeds at horizon 18,000 AND win a majority of seeds.

   arm: survival ticks, deaths by cause (eaten / starved / aged), fleet size + mean energy
   trajectory, income/burn, and the fraction of agent-ticks spent below the 100-energy lockout.
   Runs on Linux (the grader's platform) because rankings are platform-local.
3. **Implement the evasion mechanism** (off-by-default flags, byte-identical when off):
   retreat-while-facing — move backwards away from the predator while turning to keep it inside
   my front 90 degrees (which is *predicted* to suppress its charge; the size of the effect must
   be MEASURED, see step 2's closing-rate diagnostic — do not infer it from the source), at a
   speed above its sprint of 15, and only while `energy >= evade_energy_min`; never sprint below
   100 (the sim's own lockout).
4. **Test E arms paired vs H1 at horizon 18,000 on 20 held-out seeds** (baseline / E1 / E2 / E3,
   sweeping trigger radius and energy reserve). Kill criterion: if no arm beats the bar, park
   evasion and record it as a measured negative.
5. **Implement the endgame thin relay** (off by default): after a REACHABLE trigger, spawn only
   when an agent holds >=250 energy, cap the fleet at 3, and let natural age mortality thin it.
   Include the baseline arm in the same run (the earlier refutation violated this rule).
6. **Implement selfish-herd positioning** as its own arm: when a predator is visible and a
   conspecific is closer to it than I am, keep foraging; if I am the closest, move to put a
   conspecific between us. Ablate separately, never bundled.
7. **Combined paired sweep** of the surviving arms (plus the cheap biome-preference arm) on 20
   FRESH held-out seeds at horizon 18,000.
8. **Launch the MAP-Elites QD search** over the full parameter vector including the new
   switches, 5+ resampled seeds per generation, incumbent protected in the archive, on the VPS
   with `--cpus=3` and endpoint latency measured before and after. Checkpoint rule announced in
   advance: if the best is still inside the noise band of the incumbent after 8 generations,
   stop and report the plateau. Runs overnight while the endpoint serves.
9. **Time-boxed ML track**: RL evasion sub-policy reusing `rl_fleet.py`, reward = the sim's own
   score (survival time) + fruit/1000 - eaten penalty (the SELFish reward design), hybrid
   heuristic spawn, curriculum starting mid-episode where the deaths actually happen. Kill
   criterion: if income and survival have not both moved within the box, park it and say so.
10. **Validate and deploy**: on any arm clearing the bar — update the sha256 ledger,
    `tools/check_controller.py --update`, deploy to Helsinki (user approval), verify `/predict`
    and provenance, hand the user a validation to queue, and keep a documented one-command

  pre-spawn energy is below ~200. Now worded as "spawning can push the parent below the
  100-energy sprint threshold".
- "The score is simply time alive" — INCOMPLETE. Small fruit/energy terms exist (full fruit
  +0.06 vs 0.1/tick; being eaten up to -5). Now worded as "survival time dominates".
- "The endgame is a banked coast, not a forage" — TOO CONFIDENT. Earlier measurements found
  substantial fruit standing in the world while agents struggled to access it. Reframed.
- "Facing makes predators close 40% slower" — UNVERIFIED, and the arithmetic I quoted was wrong:
  the pivot's radial ratio is cos(45 deg) = 0.707 (~29%), before the agent's own motion is
  counted. This will be MEASURED, not inferred. Step 2 gains a closing-rate diagnostic split by
  whether the agent was facing the predator.
- "MAP-Elites is designed for our ±1,000-tick noise" — MISCHARACTERISED. Evaluation is
  deterministic inside a fixed harness (462.3278 reproduces exactly); the dominant variance is
  SEED-TO-SEED outcome variance. QD is justified by diversity/robustness across seeds, and by
  keeping alternatives alive.
- "The safest position is having another agent between you and the predator" — NOT ESTABLISHED

- "Reproduction + the sprint lockout explains the ~7k ceiling" — NOT CONCLUDED. Known: low energy
